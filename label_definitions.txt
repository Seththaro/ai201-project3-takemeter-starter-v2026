# Copied from the README's Label Taxonomy so the baseline is judged on exactly
# the definitions in my submission. baseline.py reads this file.

analysis: a Hacker News comment that supports its point with at least one specific, checkable item - a number, date, named source or link, direct quote, or concrete technical detail
hot_take: a confident opinion, prediction or claim with nothing checkable behind it, including rhetorical questions and personal anecdotes with no verifiable detail
reaction: a short emotional or social response - thanks, congratulations, a joke, sarcasm, a quip, or bare agreement - that does not make a claim of its own
question: a comment whose main purpose is to ask a real question and get information, an example or a clarification back
